## Summary

A literature review on whether large language models can be used to simulate society. It covers:

- the history and uses of society simulation in the social sciences, and traditional simulation tools;
- architectures for LLM-based simulations, how such simulations are classified, and the technical
  challenges and limitations of LLMs;
- ways to adapt LLMs: RAG, fine-tuning and prompt engineering;
- an analysis of existing systems, including *Generative Agents* ("Interactive simulacra of human
  behaviour", Park et al., 2023) and *AgentSociety* (Piao et al., 2025).

This work has no code. The practical system was built in the
[practical course work](../2_practical_course_work_2025-26/).
